# Privacy Policy for inworked

**Last updated:** November 24, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **inworked** (also called "Shaot"; "we", "our", or "the App"), the free payroll and working-hours tracker available at [inworked.com](https://inworked.com), handles information when you use the website and the installed web app.

inworked is developed and operated by Osman Ali Kaçar under the Benosso brand. Contact: **support@benosso.com**.

## 2. No Account, Data Stays on Your Device

inworked does not require registration, a name, an email address or a password.

Everything you enter — working hours, notes, employers, hourly rates, tax settings, payslip figures and expenses — is stored **only in your own browser on your own device** (browser storage such as localStorage / IndexedDB). We do not receive or store this data unless you choose to use the optional sync feature described below.

You can delete all of your data at any time by using the reset option in the App's settings or by clearing your browser's site data.

## 3. Information We Process

### 3.1 Optional device sync code
If you create a sync code to move your data to another device, a copy of your App data is uploaded to our server and stored under that 6-character code for up to **365 days**, so you can download it on another device. Anyone who knows the code can download that copy, so keep it private. The copy expires automatically after 365 days. You can ask us to delete it earlier by emailing us with the code.

### 3.2 Usage counter
To count how many people use the App, your browser creates a **random device identifier** that is not linked to your name, email or any other personal information. The server adds it to anonymous counting structures (HyperLogLog) that can only return totals (total visits, unique devices and devices active today) and cannot be used to read the identifier back.

### 3.3 Invite counter
If you share an invite link, we only store a counter of how many times your invite code was used. No personal data is attached to it.

### 3.4 Job listings
When you open the job listings section, your search is forwarded to our job-listings provider (CareerJet). As that provider requires, the request includes your IP address and browser user-agent. We do not store them.

### 3.5 Server logs
Our hosting provider (Vercel) may automatically log technical data such as IP address, browser type and request time to operate and secure the service. These logs are kept for a limited period and are not used to identify you.

### 3.6 Camera
The barcode scanner in the shopping-list section uses your camera only after you give permission. Images are processed on your device and are never uploaded.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App loads data or code from the following third parties. Each processes your IP address as part of the normal operation of the internet:

- **Google AdSense** — displays advertisements (see Section 5).
- **Hebcal** (hebcal.com) — Jewish holiday and Shabbat times for the city you select.
- **open.er-api.com** — currency exchange rates.
- **CareerJet** — job listings.
- **cdnjs, unpkg, jsDelivr** — delivery of open-source libraries.
- **Vercel** — website hosting.

## 5. Advertising and Cookies (Google AdSense)

We use **Google AdSense** to show ads, which helps keep inworked free.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- For more information about how Google uses data, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

inworked itself does not set advertising cookies, and we do not share the data you enter in the App (hours, salary and so on) with advertisers.

## 6. Children

inworked is intended for working adults. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 7. Your Rights

Because your App data is stored on your own device, you have direct control over it: you can view, export, change or delete it at any time. For questions about the sync copy or any other request, contact **support@benosso.com**.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
