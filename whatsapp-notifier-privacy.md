# Privacy Policy for Benosso WhatsApp Notifier

**Last updated: 8 September 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes what data Benosso WhatsApp Notifier ("the App") accesses, why, and how it is handled. The App is installed by a Wix site owner ("the Merchant") to send WhatsApp notifications about their own orders. Provider: Benosso İnternet Yazılım İthalat İhracat Ticaret ve Bilgi Teknolojileri A.Ş. ("Benosso"), a company registered in Türkiye (tax no. 1640774681), benosso.com, support@benosso.com.

## 1. Data the App accesses
From the Merchant's Wix site, only to provide its function:
- **Order data** — order number, total, currency, items, and order status. Used to compose the notification message.
- **Buyer contact details** — name and phone number from the order. Used to send the customer their order confirmation, and only when the Merchant enables customer messages.
- **Abandoned checkout data** — buyer name, phone and recovery link, used only for the cart-recovery message when the Merchant enables that feature.
- **App settings** — the Merchant's own configuration (Meta credentials, store name, language, message preferences), stored in the site's own Wix CMS collections.

The App does **not** access payment card data, passwords, or any data unrelated to sending order notifications.

## 2. How data is used
- To send the Merchant an alert for each new order.
- To send the customer an order confirmation, when enabled by the Merchant.
- To send a cart-recovery message, when enabled by the Merchant.

Messages are delivered through the **official WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta Cloud API)** using the Merchant's own WhatsApp Business account and credentials. Benosso does not send messages on its own behalf and does not use the data for its own marketing.

## 3. Where data is stored
- **Settings and counters** live in the Merchant's own site (Wix CMS collections). They stay with the site.
- **Message content** is transmitted to Meta for delivery and is subject to Meta's own terms and privacy policy.
- The App keeps no separate customer database outside the Merchant's site.

## 4. Credentials
The Merchant's Meta access token is stored in the site's own collection and is **never returned to the browser**; the dashboard only shows whether a token is saved. It is used solely to call the Meta Cloud API on the Merchant's behalf.

## 5. Retention and deletion
Settings and send counters are removed when the App is uninstalled, or earlier on request. Requests: **support@benosso.com** — actioned within 30 days.

## 6. Sharing
Data is shared only with **Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform)** for message delivery. It is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone else.

## 7. Merchant responsibility
The Merchant is responsible for having a lawful basis to message their customers and for complying with WhatsApp's Business Messaging Policy and applicable privacy and marketing laws.
